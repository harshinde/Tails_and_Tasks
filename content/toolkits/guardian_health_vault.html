Small daily habits protect a long, happy life.

Longevity is rarely about dramatic overhauls. It comes from noticing small changes early, keeping records in one place, and repeating simple preventative routines.

This kit gives you a clear home for their medical baseline, nutrition, and five-minute wellness habits. Take it one checkbox at a time. You’ve got this.

1. The Longevity Mindset

Preventative care starts with active observation. Three habits that protect long-term health:

  • Know their baseline — check energy, coat, gum color, and appetite regularly so subtle changes stand out.
  • Weigh-ins matter — a monthly weigh-in catches drift before it becomes joint stress or energy loss.
  • Pace, don’t rush — change food portions or exercise over 7–10 days so their body can adapt.

4. Daily Preventative Routines

Five-minute practices for the top longevity priorities from The Guardian path.

1. Dental care & hygiene

Why it matters: Mouth health supports whole-body wellness over time.

  • Days 1–3: let them taste pet-safe toothpaste from your finger.
  • Days 4–7: gently rub outer teeth with a soft finger brush or gauze.
  • Reward right after every short session.
  • Aim for ~30 seconds, a few times a week — consistency beats perfection.

2. Joint mobility & soft movement

Why it matters: Comfortable joints keep them active and playful longer.

  • Add rug runners on slick floors to reduce slipping.
  • Warm up with 2 minutes of easy walking before fetch or hard play.
  • For seniors: short walks, gentle rear-leg massage, no forced stretching.
  • Stop if they tense, limp, or disengage — comfort first.

3. Digestion & weight management

Why it matters: Staying near ideal weight supports energy, joints, and lifespan.

  • Measure meals (scale or marked scoop) instead of guessing.
  • Swap some high-calorie treats for pet-safe, lower-calorie options your vet is happy with.
  • Log one weigh-in each month on the ledger page.
  • Change portions gradually over 7–10 days if you adjust food.
